Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Pane: Remote Desktop for Mac

The short version: Pane has no account and no server of its own. Your hosts, settings and passwords stay on your device. The app connects directly to your computers. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous usage analytics, and you can see exactly what that includes below.

Who we are

Pane is made by Understudios (Jan Jiráň), Czech Republic. If you have any question about this policy, write to jan.jiran@understudios.org.

No account

Pane does not ask you to sign up or sign in. We do not know who you are, and we have no user database.

What stays on your device

  • Saved hosts and settings. The names, addresses, ports and preferences of the computers you add are stored only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Usernames and passwords. Credentials for your computers are stored only in the device Keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. They never leave the device and are never included in analytics.
  • Screen content. What you see on the remote screen is never stored, with one exception: Pane can keep an optional last-frame thumbnail of a host so you recognise it on the home screen. That thumbnail is kept on the device only and is deleted when you remove the host.

Direct connections, no relay

Pane connects directly from your device to your computer using VNC (the protocol behind macOS Screen Sharing), over your local network, a VPN such as Tailscale, or any address you provide. There is no Understudios relay, proxy or cloud service in between. Your session traffic is exchanged only between your device and your computer.

Analytics

To understand which features are used and where connections fail, Pane sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, hosted on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

What we collect:

  • App opened
  • Connection attempted, succeeded or failed (with a generic error category)
  • Feature used (for example keyboard shown, display switched, trackpad mode)
  • Session length, in coarse buckets
  • Device model, OS version and app version

What we never collect:

  • Hostnames or IP addresses of your computers
  • Usernames or passwords
  • Screen content, keystrokes or clipboard content

Your device’s IP address is discarded at ingestion and is not stored with events. Events are identified by a random identifier that is not linked to you. We do not use advertising identifiers, we show no advertising, and we never sell data. Analytics data is retained for 12 months and then deleted.

Permissions Pane asks for

PermissionWhy
Local NetworkRequired to connect to computers on your Wi-Fi and to discover them. Without it, Pane can only reach computers over a VPN or the internet.
BonjourUsed to find Macs that advertise Screen Sharing on the local network so you can add them by name.
Location (while using)iOS requires location access to reveal the name of the Wi-Fi network you are on. Pane uses it only to display that name next to your hosts. Your location is never stored and never sent anywhere.
Face ID / Touch IDUnlocks the credentials stored in the Keychain. Biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave and is not available to Pane.

You can change any of these at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Children

Pane is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
